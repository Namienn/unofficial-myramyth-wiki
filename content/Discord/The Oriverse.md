## Levelling System

Within the Oriverse discord server, there is a levelling system based on different interactions possible within the server, like sending messages, staying in voice chat or using certain commands. It is stated that none of the clues only accessible through experience gain are necessary for comprehension of the universe, but they still pose questions in their own regard

### Levelling Channels

The most relevant bits of information regarding the levelling system lie mostly in the channels you unlock through it. 

Whenever you reach the level threshold for these and get added, someone will repeat a predetermined sentence to you.
