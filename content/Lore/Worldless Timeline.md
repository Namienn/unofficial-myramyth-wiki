# Briefing

Worldless is one of the six main [[S-X Timelines]], introduced through the [[Short Stories]] as S-X.2

In this timeline, a great portion of [[Pia]] around the region named [[Thoxica]] is tainted by the [[Sludge]], having changed the flora and fauna completely, at the same time it created a disease that transformed all living creatures into abominations warped by the Sludge.

[[Uto]] is the only human settlement untouched by the Sludge

## Uto

[[Uto]] is, as explained by Oliver in [[Short Story - Worldless]] and more thoroughly explored in [[Short Story - Message]], the only human settlement still untouched by the [[Sludge]]

The researchers there are searching for healthy young people under the pretense of finding a way to cure or counteract the corruption of the Sludge, doing something akin to brainwashing related to pink crystals, as seen by Oliver in Message

[[Subject 3310]] and [[Subject 7302]] are both deemed deviances from the norm, however, due to 3310's unresponsiveness to the experiments and 7302's attachment to 3310
