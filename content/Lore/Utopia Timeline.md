# Briefing

Utopia is one of the six main [[S-X Timelines]], introduced through the [[Short Stories]] as S-X.2.1

In this timeline, [[Uto]] and [[Pia]] are both in good state, showing no major signs of [[Sludge]] corruption nor having suffered any noteworthy losses from the [[Phexython]]
