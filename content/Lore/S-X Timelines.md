# Briefing

The Myramyth universe is composed of many timelines intertwined by a common origin and a mysterious event that generated their divide.

The timelines are introduced and developed upon in a more concrete manner by the [[Short Stories]] introduced in the [[Myramyth Info Page]]

These timelines are:
* [[Revelations Timeline|Revelations (Timeline)]]
* [[Hyperion Timeline|Hyperion (Timeline)]]
* [[Worldless Timeline|Worldless (Timeline)]]
* [[Perfection Timeline|Perfection (Timeline)]]
* [[Utopia Timeline|Utopia (Timeline)]]
* [[Dystopia Timeline]]

# Bonds

### Myramyth Website

The timelines are introduced alongside [[The Clones]] in the [[Myramyth Info Page]]

### S-X Versions

The timelines are described as different "Versions" of the S-X world, in the following fashion:

* S-X.0: [[Revelations Timeline]]
	* S-X.0.1: [[Perfection Timeline]]
* S-X.1: [[Hyperion Timeline]]
	* S-X.1.1: [[Dystopia Timeline]]
* S-X.2: [[Worldless Timeline]]
	* S-X.2.1 [[Utopia Timeline]]

### Alyx Ploht

[[Alyx Ploht]] is one of the common factors across all timelines, having survived in some and died on others.

More details about them are linked in their page
