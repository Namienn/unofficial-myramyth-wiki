# Briefing

Perfection is one of the six main [[S-X Timelines]], introduced through the [[Short Stories]] as S-X.0.1

In this timeline, [[Pia]] has been completely ravaged by the [[Phexython]], having it be lured into [[The Aether]] by this world's [[Alyx Ploht S-X.0.1|Alyx Ploht]] lead every living soul in that universe be assimilated by it. There is no direct mention of [[Uto]]

## The Clones

This timeline is watched by [[Okifaun]], as seen in [[Short Story - Perfection]]

## Alyx Ploht

[[Alyx Ploht S-X.0.1]] is dead after the event that led to the endangerment of the universe, having been the one to suggest the solution to it anyway, as seen in [[Short Story - Perfection]]

## Perfection

[[Perfection]] is said to be the sole survivor of the Phexython event that destroyed the world for an undisclosed reason, apparently always aware it would happen. Through that, she achieved a form of godhood

[[Short Story - Bartender]] shows a few results of Perfection's godly endeavors after the conclusion of her investigation of the [[Leylines]], showing life and vegetation otherwise alien to [[Pia]]
