# Briefing

Hyperion is one of the six main [[S-X Timelines]], introduced through the [[Short Stories]] as S-X.2.1

In this timeline, [[Pia]] has been destroyed by the [[Phexython]], [[Uto]] not being mentioned at all. The stories in this universe revolve around colonies in [[New Pia]]

# Bonds

## Short Stories

Here are the [[Short Stories]] that happen within Hyperion:

* [[Short Story - Hyperion]]
* [[Short Story - Discovery]]

Within the short stories, the dating system of this timeline follows the expression `<eon>E-<megaannum>-<millenia>-<year>`, Which is likely in reference to the time spent since the estimated beginning of the universe

## The Clones

This timeline is watched by [[Oribot]], as seen in [[Short Story - Hyperion]]

## Alyx Ploht

[[Alyx Ploht S-X.1]] is still alive, having utilized technology to slow down her own aging as explained in [[Short Story - Discovery]]

Also in [[Short Story - Discovery]], it is explained that [[Alyx Ploht S-X.2.1]] has been visiting other timelines to inspect their current condition

## Mana's Disappearance

In this universe, [[Mana]] seems to no longer exist, or at least be accessible by usual means, according to what is explained in [[Short Story - Hyperion]]

However, at [[Short Story - Discovery]], [[Alyx Ploht S-X.2.1]]'s arcane gear is able to function regardless, hinting at possible characteristics of the nature of Mana

## Phexython's Fate

In [[Short Story - Hyperion]], it is explained how the [[Phexython]] was defeated, "Killed in front of [[Angelus]]", and it's carcass "Sprawled across the area of an entire city".

From the carcass of the creature, a vast amount of technology was extracted and reverse engineered, leading to this universe's space age and the relocation to [[New Pia]]

Most notably within the short stories, [[Alyx Ploht S-X.1]] is seen studying the [[Hyperion Core]], the heart of the Phexython, supposedly the greatest energy source observed in that universe so far

## Planetary Body 0

In [[Short Story - Hyperion]], it is explained how this universe is inhabiting cities and colonies floating above a new planet, referred to as PB0, or [[New Pia]].

The planet's surface is under a terraforming process, overseen by [[Oribot]]
