# Briefing

This is the first Short Story occurring within [[Dystopia Timeline]], introducing [[Cyreil Windkeeper]], [[Nylo]], [[Alyx Ploht S-X.1.1]] and [[Aritsuki]]

# Bonds

## Magic War

In this short story, we are introduced to the fact that [[Dystopia Timeline]]'s world is driven by a war that isn't explained in detail

During the invasion attempted later on in the story, Cyreil is shown to have heard of [[Angelus]]'s doings, likely an enemy to his cause

## Cyreil's Mana Attunement

This short story introduces Cyreil's deep connection to [[Mana]] and the [[Leylines]], describing his ability to understand the whispers of the energy around him in a fashion similar to how [[Primordial Language]] is described

It is also shown how Cyreil has an inclination towards Cryomancy, often denying orders to use other sorts of magic to favor his own competences

## Griffon's Peak

In the short story, Cyreil and Nylo arrive in [[Griffon's Peak]], finding an assortment of items of unknown origin and belonging. 

One of those items is in line with what is known of the [[Godslayer Sword]] according to what is described of it later on in the story

Another one of the items is a silk cloak that manifests itself as a set of silver tattoos that whispers in Primordial, showing will of it's own and ulterior motives

## Leylines

Through Cyreil's eyes, the [[Leylines]] are explained to pile upon what are called Mana Fluxes, places that are marked by [[Chaos]] for whatever reason

## Bridge of the Gods

The mission Cyreil is sent to within the short story happens on the [[Bridge of the Gods]], said to span over a hundred meters over a river. No mention is made of the [[The Abyss]]

## Alyx Ploht

In this universe, [[Alyx Ploht S-X.1.1]] is shown to not only be alive, but also be the lover of [[Cyreil Windkeeper]]
