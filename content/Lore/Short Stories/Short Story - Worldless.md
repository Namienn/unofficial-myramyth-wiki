# Briefing

This is the first Short Story occurring within [[Worldless Timeline|Worldless (Timeline)]], introducing [[Wendi]], [[Oliver]] and [[Alyx Ploht S-X.2]]'s fate

# Bonds

## Alyx Ploht

At the beginning of the short story, the description of the body devoured by Wendi fits the description of [[Alyx Ploht]], hinting at the fact that [[Alyx Ploht S-X.2]] is very likely dead within this timeline

## Wendi

In this short story, we see the world surrounding [[Wendi]], a middle aged woman tainted by the [[Sludge]], having become on of the [[Sludges]] herself.

Through her eyes, many different aspects of the world and experience surrounding the Sludges are explained, including but not limited to:
* Enhanced physical capabilities
* Extreme disfigurement of the body
* Intense and constant need for food
* A Sludge's apparent lack of consciousness, Wendi herself being an exception
* Sensing of living beings through [[Mana]] and [[Leylines]]

## Thoxica's Crater

This short story introduces the state of [[Pia]], mentioning a crater within [[Thoxica]] as the ground zero for the beginning of the [[Sludge]]'s infection of the planet

## Uto

This short story introduces the state of [[Uto]] as being the last surviving settlement for scientific advancements and inhabitation within this universe, looking for healthy survivors of the Sludge to help with research regarding it
