# Briefing

This is the second Short Story occurring within [[Hyperion Timeline]], officially introducing the core of the [[S-X Timelines]] and explaining the meaning of [[The Crux Event]]

# Bonds

## Alyx Meetup

[[Alyx Ploht S-X.1]] Meets with [[Alyx Ploht S-X.2.1]] right after [[Short Story - Hyperion]] left off

This is also the first time Alyx's surname is explicitly stated on a Short Story, as [[Oribot]] utilizes it to discern between both versions of Alyx

Further down the line, Alyx 2.1 confirms that she is from [[Utopia Timeline|Utopia (Timeline)]]

## Timeline Distinctions

[[Alyx Ploht S-X.2.1]] Explains that the fate of the [[Phexython]] is one of the main things that discerns one timeline from another. 

Here, they explain that [[Utopia Timeline|Utopia (Timeline)]] is suffering from the Phexython's corruption in a manner similar to [[Worldless Timeline|Worldless (Timeline)]], but still hasn't succeeded to the same degree

## Oribot's Hidden Capabilities

In the end of this Short Story, [[Oribot]] is shown to be much more capable than previously stated, responsible of things outside the knowledge of [[Alyx Ploht S-X.1]] and possibly [[Alyx Ploht S-X.2.1]]
