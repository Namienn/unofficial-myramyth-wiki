# Bonds

## Max's Torn Perception

Max is shown to be able to naturally converse with a different plane of existence within this universe, to the point of being unable to tell apart what belongs to one or the other despite knowing that there must be a divide.

Due to that, Max has his own method of categorizing phenomena he perceives around him:
* [[The Gowai]], which he describes as follows:
  > 'The Gowai housed the humans that constantly say, “Go away”'
* [[The Cohere]], which he describes as follows:
  > 'the Cohere felt more cohesive and recognizable, also with humans that say “Come here” a lot.'
  
Notably, he is able to perceive [[Occulants]], to the point of doing a presentation of their history

As an experiment, he makes a drawing of himself with things strictly from either side as an attempt at making something both sides would see as the same thing, which [[Tori]] says that she will "show the others"

This story shows [[Alyx Ploht S-X.0]] as a figurine made by [[Max]], having fallen over from his shelf and lost her head. This likely points to the fact that this Alyx only exists as what Max calls the Cohere
