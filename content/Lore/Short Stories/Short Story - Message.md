# Briefing

This is the second Short Story occurring within [[Worldless Timeline|Worldless (Timeline)]], introducing this timeline's [[Uto]]

# Bonds

## Oliver's Flame

Contrary to what has been shown so far about [[The Fire]] of other clones, [[Oliver]] is able to conjure and put away his at his own discretion, allowing him to move wherever he wishes

This might be an ability of other [[The Clones]], but it was first shown in this story

## Walteria

Once [[Oliver]] arrives at [[Uto]], he is introduced to be at [[Walteria]]. It seems to be a sort of experimental complex with infrastructure created to sustain the experiments happening within it

It also houses the scientists within it, alongside children and teenagers that roam the area freely

## Experimentation

Within [[Walteria]], Oliver follows two researchers to the cafeteria of the place, where most of the children are

Each one of them is wearing a gray skin-tight suit and an apparatus holding a pink crystal inside. Every child seems to be hypnotized or brainwashed in some fashion, except for [[Subject 3310]] and [[Subject 7302]]
