# Briefing

This is the first Short Story occurring within [[Hyperion Timeline]], introducing [[Alyx Ploht S-X.1]] and [[Oribot]]

# Bonds

## Mana's Disappearance

In this short story, it is introduced that [[Mana]] no longer exists in this world, having been extinguished sometime in the past

## Alyx Ploht

This story shows how [[Alyx Ploht S-X.1]] is still alive, living in recluse from civilization and around machines and androids, including [[Oribot]], while she researches the remnants of the [[Phexython]]'s body, most notably the [[Hyperion Core]]

## Phexython's Fate

This story talks about the fate of this universe's [[Phexython]], said to have died in front of [[Angelus]] and have it's body cover the area of a city

It's body was then the source of many technological wonders as it's materials were utilized in new applications and it's technology was reverse engineered for utilization by Planetary Body 0's population

## Planetary Body 0

Planetary Body 0 is this universe's nomenclature for [[Pia]], the name having been granted after the event with the [[Phexython]] completely destroyed the planet's surface and gave rise to the technological revolution that characterizes this world

Due to those events, they seem to have moved to another planet, called PB0 or [[New Pia]] and begun efforts of terraforming upon it's surface 

## Another Alyx

In the end of this short story, A new [[Alyx Ploht]] appeared out of the blue. This is likely [[Alyx Ploht S-X.2.1]] due to her highly complex equipment, recorded history of being aware of other timelines and possibly able to traverse them
