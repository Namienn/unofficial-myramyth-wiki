# Briefing

This is the second Short Story occurring within [[Revelations Timeline]], introducing [[The Doctor]]

# Bonds

## Communication

In this short story, it is shown that [[Tori]] is said to be in charge of communication as opposed to talking to the fire.

It isn't made clear in the narrative what this is

## Max's Dreams

[[Max]]'s dreams are introduced as usually being a expanse of swirling and changing colors, basic and unconforming as opposed to the real world's reliance of expectation and opinion

[[The Doctor]] explains how this expanse isn't Max's mind, but something beyond that

Upon that statement, Max's dream shifts to one where time flies by as a forest grows before his eyes, an [[Occulants|Occulant]] staring him down as it does


## The Doctor

[[Max]] lands on a metal platform that floated in the sky above what seems to be either [[Griffon's Peak]] or the river running underneath the [[Bridge of the Gods]]. He looks around to find many meters-long piles of unreadable records and "junk".

Soon, [[The Doctor]] rises alongside [[Buaris]], observing records the robot printed under his command. Not long after, something falls from the sky, and Max is sent on another time sprint before he could understand what it was

Upon questioning, the Doctor answered: "I'm giving you clarity"
Further questioning leads to the following:
> “I created many things, but it all led to only one creation,” the Doctor spoke while tapping on the screen of the robot. The robot’s screen was black with white text sprawling across. “It was all for this.”
> “This was the crux event I manufactured.”

Furthermore, the object that landed follows the familiar description of the [[Phexython]], similarities that [[Max]] was able to acknowledge as he is woken up by [[Tori]] and her weasel

As such, this short story connects [[The Doctor]] to [[Phexython]] and [[The Crux Event]] that led to the formation of the [[S-X Timelines]]

## Tori's Weasel

As Max was having the unusual dream with [[The Doctor]], Tori's weasel seemed to be distressed with something and trying to warn it's caretaker of something.

It's unclear how or why it did it, but there's a very heavy implication that the weasel somehow knew of Max's condition as it was happening
