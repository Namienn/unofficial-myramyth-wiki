# Briefing

This is the second Short Story occurring within [[Dystopia Timeline]], introducing new aspects about the characters within it

# Bonds

## Cyreil's Hand Runes

It is briefly mentioned how Cyreil has runes scarring his hands, unclear how he got them, why he has them or what they do

It's possible that it is linked to his Cryomancy

## Cyreil's Weave

In the beginning of the short story, [[Cyreil Windkeeper]]'s silver tattoos began to talk to him, showing visions of dead bodies while talking 

The people shown as the enemies wear improvised clothing made from pieces of scrap cloth and rudimentary equipment, as opposed to his and his allies' magical gear

## Cyreil's Flashback

Cyreil is reminded of something once he spots [[Aritsuki]] inspecting a hole in the bridge:
> "the woman falling in the water, the boy crashing through the bridge, the giant platform falling out of the sky; it was a blur at this point. The blood in the water ran thick through her eyes."

It's probably in reference to [[The Crux Event]], as it mentions the platform inhabited by [[The Doctor]]

## Phexython's Containment

[[Cyreil Windkeeper|Cyreil]] and [[Aritsuki]] talk about the [[Phexython]]'s carcass, which is hidden in this world. Their plan to contain it isn't shown to us, but it possibly involves freezing the carcass

[[Alyx Ploht S-X.1.1]] is likely to be trying to harness it, to the apparent dismay of Aritsuki

## Fire's Message

In the end of the Short Story, it is mentioned that some version of [[Alyx Ploht]] would be slain by the [[Godslayer Sword]], but it is unclear what it means
