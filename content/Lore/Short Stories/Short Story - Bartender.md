## New Lifeforms

In this short story, we are introduced to new forms of life, previously unmentioned on the stories. Mother and child, they talk about the world around them as if created by a god after looking for something for so long

The myth told by the mother is a reflection of [[Perfection]]'s history within the universe

Beyond that, new trees and vegetation is seen on the background, though there isn't any signs of animal activity beyond the story's two focused elements

## Perfection's Indifference

When faced with her creation, [[Perfection]] is quick to dismiss anything either of the two figures stood for, dismantling them into ashes before they could react. Perfection's indifference stretches further to a dismissal of [[Okifaun]]'s comments, focused on an unspecified goal
