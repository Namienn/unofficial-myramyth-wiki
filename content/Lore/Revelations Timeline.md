# Briefing

Revelations is one of the six main [[S-X Timelines]], introduced through the [[Short Stories]] as S-X.0

In this timeline, [[Pia]] seems to be in a good state, the [[Phexython]] and everything related to [[Mana]] seemingly existing on another plane of existence only directly perceived by [[Max]]. There's no mention of [[Uto]] or the [[Sludge]]

# Bonds

## Short Stories

Here are the [[Short Stories]] that happen within Revelations:

* [[Short Story - Revelations]]
* [[Short Story - Dream]]

Within the short stories, the dating system of this timeline follows the expression `<year>`, Following a similar date to real life as the year listed in both Revelations and Dream is 2024

## The Clones

This timeline is watched by [[Tori]], as seen in [[Short Story - Revelations]]

## Alyx Ploht

[[Alyx Ploht S-X.0]] seems to be something that exists only within [[Max]]'s psyche, as she is seen as a model in [[Short Story - Revelations]]

## Max's Torn Perception

[[Short Story - Revelations]] tells us that [[Max]] perceives the universe in two different halves:

* [[The Gowai]], which he describes as follows:
  > 'The Gowai housed the humans that constantly say, “Go away”'
* [[The Cohere]], which he describes as follows:
  > 'the Cohere felt more cohesive and recognizable, also with humans that say “Come here” a lot.'

It is heavily implied that everything involving [[Mana]] is only present in [[The Cohere]], that seems to only be perceived by Max in this world

## The Doctor

In [[Short Story - Dream]], [[Max]] is introduced to [[The Doctor]] as it explains key points regarding [[The Crux Event]] and it's relationship to the [[Phexython]]
