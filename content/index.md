Welcome to the Unofficial Myramyth Wiki!

This is an effort (so far spearheaded by Namienn but with plans to expand eventually) to make a functioning wiki for the Myramyth universe, serving as a common base of knowledge about everything seen within it

> [!warning] Spoiler Warning
> Most of this wiki explains many aspects of the Myramyth universe that would be otherwise hidden, doing little to hide solutions to puzzles and mysteries. If you wish to investigate Myramyth on your own, this is your warning to leave

This wiki is under active development as a baseline investigation is made upon all that is laid out so far, so it finds itself very incomplete at the present moment. thank you for your understanding
